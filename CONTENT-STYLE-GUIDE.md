# CONTENT STYLE GUIDE: Lotus Editorial Standards

All automated drafting in Lotus is governed by strict linguistic rules to ensure a calm, objective, and high-density editorial voice.

---

## 1. Word Budgets (Non-Negotiable)

| Field | Hard Limit | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Headline** | **Max 12 words** | High-density statement of entity and core event |
| **Teaser** | **Max 20 words (1 sentence)** | Visible summary sentence on collapsed card |
| **What It Is** | **Max 25 words** | Objective definition of product or technology |
| **Why It Matters** | **Max 30 words** | Strategic implication for markets or industry |
| **Bank Angle** | **Max 25 words** | Direct implication for financial institutions |
| **Today in One Sentence** | **Max 25 words** | Masthead summary capturing the day's overarching theme |
| **Spotlight Pitch** | **Max 2 sentences** | Concise elevator overview |

---

## 2. Word Count Target Ranges

- **Default Browse Mode:** **500 to 700 words** total visible text across the entire Today page.
- **Full Read Mode (10-minute read):** **2,000 to 2,500 words** total when all details cards are expanded.

---

## 3. Prohibited Hype Words (Immediate Rejection)

The validator immediately flags and rejects items containing empty sensationalism:
- `game-changer` / `game changer`
- `revolutionary`
- `unprecedented`
- `disruptive`
- `groundbreaking`
- `supercharge`
- `rockstar` / `ninja`
- `unicorn`
- `skyrocket`
- `10x`
- `paradigm shift`
- `silver bullet`
- `next-gen`
- `state-of-the-art`
- `cutting-edge`
- `killer app`
- `jaw-dropping`

---

## 4. Tone Principles

1. **Active Voice & Factual Restraint:** Prefer "Acme raises $15M to expand SEPA rails" over "Exciting times as Acme is set to disrupt the payments landscape."
2. **Attributed Uncertainty:** If a metric is claimed by the startup, write *"the company says"* or *"reported by TechCrunch."* Never state self-reported metrics as verified independent facts.
3. **No Financial Advice:** Never recommend buying, investing, or underwriting. State observations as market dynamics.

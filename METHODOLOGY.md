# EDITORIAL METHODOLOGY & SOURCING

Lotus follows strict factual journalism standards, combining autonomous data collection with disciplined analytical filters.

---

## 1. Sourcing Protocol

- **8 Core Sources Monitored Daily:**
  1. Y Combinator Companies Directory & Announcements
  2. Product Hunt Daily Standouts
  3. TechCrunch Venture & Fintech Feeds
  4. Crunchbase News Funding Disclosures
  5. Trend Hunter Emergent Consumer Signals
  6. EU-Startups European Venture Activity
  7. Springwise Sustainable & Impact Innovation
  8. Exploding Topics Breakout Search Trends
- **Bonus Feeds:** Finextra, Sifted, FinTech Futures.
- **Strict Compliance:** Only authorized public RSS/Atom feeds and non-authenticated endpoints are polled. Zero paywall circumvention or aggressive bot behavior.

---

## 2. Signal Scoring Engine

Every ingested item is assigned a weighted composite signal score between 0 and 100:

$$\text{Signal Score} = 0.30 \times \text{Novelty} + 0.25 \times \text{Significance} + 0.20 \times \text{Cross-Mentions} + 0.15 \times \text{Traction} + 0.10 \times \text{Banking Relevance}$$

- **Novelty (30%):** Does this represent a fundamentally new business model or technical architecture rather than an incremental product tweak?
- **Significance (25%):** Sourced capital scale, lead venture syndicate, or critical regulatory designation.
- **Cross-Source Mentions (20%):** Number of distinct publishers independently reporting on this company or event.
- **Traction (15%):** Verifiable metric evidence (user numbers, customer logos, ARR if disclosed, transaction volume).
- **Banking Relevance (10%):** Relevance to payments, deposit gathering, lending underwriting, core modernizations, or model governance.

---

## 3. Bank Lens Formulation

To provide high value for commercial and banking readers, every daily edition formulates 4 specific strategic perspectives:
1. **Commercial Opportunity:** Where a bank can underwrite, syndicate, or capture new payment volume.
2. **Disintermediation Threat:** Emerging fintech rails threatening traditional interchange, deposit float, or advisory margins.
3. **Partnership Angle:** Co-branding, referral, or BaaS sponsorship opportunities.
4. **Regulatory & Governance:** Model risk governance (SR 11-7), compliance sandboxes, or consumer protection compliance.
